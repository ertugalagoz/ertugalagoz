# Ertuğ Alagöz

MA candidate in European History on a double-degree programme between Humboldt-Universität zu
Berlin and Università degli Studi di Roma Tor Vergata. My thesis examines Turkey's EU accession
process between 2001 and 2011.

I work on European integration, conditionality and democratic change, with a particular focus on
Turkey's relationship with the European Union. I am currently applying for doctoral positions in
Europe.

## Research projects

**[Conditionality and Its Limits](https://ertugalagoz.github.io/turkey-eu-conditionality/)**
Turkey's democratic trajectory across the accession process, 1990-2025, using the Varieties of
Democracy indices. Period trend estimation and structural break analysis, with Croatia as the
comparison case on the argument that the post-communist candidates owe their democratic levels to
1989 rather than to accession.
[Repository](https://github.com/ertugalagoz/turkey-eu-conditionality)

**[The Language of Conditionality](https://ertugalagoz.github.io/commission-language-turkiye/)**
Computational text analysis of the full run of European Commission annual reports on Türkiye,
1998-2025, measuring the Commission's own graded assessment vocabulary rather than a
researcher-built dictionary. Includes a robustness check that overturned one of the paper's own
interim claims.
[Repository](https://github.com/ertugalagoz/commission-language-turkiye)

Both are written in R and rendered with Quarto. The code and the extracted data are included in
each repository, so every figure can be reproduced from source.
